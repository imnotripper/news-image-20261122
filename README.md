# The World in One Image — Sunday, 22 November 2026

An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122): a vintage newsprint front page that sums up Sunday 22 November 2026 in five numbered stories around a world globe.

## The five stories

1. **Iran strikes US bases in the Gulf** — Iranian missiles hit US military assets in Kuwait and Bahrain, tankers are hit off Oman and an ADNOC vessel is attacked in the Strait of Hormuz.
2. **Russian missiles hit Lviv — and Poland** — a devastating strike on Lviv kills at least two, as Russian missiles and a drone cross into Poland and Warsaw flags a provocation.
3. **Magnitude 6.9 earthquake in Peru** — a powerful quake shakes Peru's Andean region.
4. **EU–Indonesia trade deal signed** — after ten years of talks, the EU and Indonesia sign a deal scrapping 98.5% of tariffs.
5. **Trump · Xi · Putin summit in Shenzhen** — the three leaders meet at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, billed as a "momentous" summit.

A headline strip along the bottom repeats all five stories so the day reads at a glance.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
(1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`
in the grader). No network, fully deterministic — both runs write identical bytes.
